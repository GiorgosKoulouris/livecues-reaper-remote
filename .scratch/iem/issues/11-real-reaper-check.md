# 11. Walk through IEM Setup on real REAPER

Status: ready-for-human
Blocked by: 05, 06, 08, 09, 10

Install on Windows and run the whole path on a copy of a real project, on a laptop or tablet, with Monitor open on a phone.

## Check

- Create, copy, rename, delete, colour and Move to end; undo each.
- Set outputs and hear the right channels. No in-ear audio reaches the master.
- Matrix cells and bulk toggles with Monitor open on that mix.
- Adopt a hand-built `IEM_` track.
- Bridge stopped: banner shown, no editing. Both bridges survive a REAPER restart.
- The grid is readable and usable on a tablet.

## Done when

Results are in `apps/iem/CONTEXT.md`, with anything not checked left under "Still unverified".
