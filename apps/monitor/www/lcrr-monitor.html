    A mix is a REAPER track set up for one musician:

    1. Name a track IEM_<name>, for example IEM_Drums.
    2. Give it at least one receive, one for each source to monitor.
    3. Give it at least one hardware output, going to the performer’s output. The overall fader controls the first one.
    4. Usually turn off its master send.

    The list updates by itself. The prefix is exactly IEM_, capitals and underscore.
